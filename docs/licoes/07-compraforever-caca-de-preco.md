## Key points

### 1. A commodity search row likely pools sellers; the buyable part is `quantity - numOwnerItems` `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=1, code read)
- The row that `C_AuctionHouse.GetCommoditySearchResultInfo` returns carries `owners`, `totalNumberOfOwners`, `numOwnerItems` and `containsOwnerItem`. Blizzard's own UI computes the buyable quantity as `searchResult.quantity - searchResult.numOwnerItems` (`Blizzard_AuctionHouseUtil.lua`, lines 217 and 241, mirror `Ketho/wow-ui-source-forever`, branch `forever`; the structure is in `Blizzard_APIDocumentationGenerated/AuctionHouseDocumentation.lua`, lines 1620 to 1633).
- A scanner that drops the whole row when `containsOwnerItem` is true goes blind to the rest of the row. The addon now keeps the row and buys the part that is not the owner's (`Scanner.lua:152`; scenario `compra-19-isca`: a row of 101 units, 1 of them the owner's, buys 100).
- **Inference, not observation in the game:** that one row joins several sellers at one price is read from the field names and from the UI's arithmetic, and so is the claim that the server skips the owner's own units on purchase. No test in the game separated them. (An episode of the night first read as "another seller posted a stack of 100 at 5s" was the owner's own stack, so it is not evidence.)
- Evidence: `gh api "repos/Ketho/wow-ui-source-forever/contents/Interface/AddOns/Blizzard_AuctionHouseUI/Shared/Blizzard_AuctionHouseUtil.lua?ref=forever" -H "Accept: application/vnd.github.raw" | grep -n numOwnerItems`.

### 2. Timings of the commodity purchase in the game `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=1 player, one night)
- Measured by the addon's own stopwatch (`CompraForeverCharDB.caca.tempos`), first reading after a streak of lost races: quote (`StartCommoditiesPurchase` to `COMMODITY_PRICE_UPDATED`) median 653 ms (532 to 803, n=15); refused purchase (press of `ConfirmCommoditiesPurchase` to the answer) median 863 ms (730 to 1,006, n=10). `C_AuctionHouse.IsThrottledMessageSystemReady()` was false at 0.5 s with the purchase pending in 10 of 10.
- Second reading, n=100 attempts (SavedVariables of 03h31, `{"moved":44,"lost":35,"ok":21}`): quote median 684 ms, successful purchase median 1,038 ms, refused purchase 850 ms. So "the answer arrives before 1 s" holds for the **refused** purchase only; the successful one is above 1 s.
- Consequence for the design: the client queue holds the next search until the answer arrives, so releasing a button 1 s after confirming would not help; the next search is chained to the press, not to a timer. A 1 s sample did not exist because the answers came back sooner.
- Owner, 2026-11-26 about 02h55 (05:55:01Z): "tomei 20 Ls seguidos. vc conseguiu coletar data para estabelecer a teoria?".
- Limits: `evidence: perishable` for the n=100 reading (the SavedVariables change every session; the end state is in the backup `%LOCALAPPDATA%\wow-agentes\backups\sessoes\2026-11-26_0339\WTF.zip`, and the n=100 output is only in the transcript). The first reading is in `CONTRATO.md:737` (section 13.4).

### 3. "Internal auction error" arrived as `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_FAILED` `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=10, one night)
- The red text "Internal auction error", seen by the owner when someone else bought between the quote and the confirmation, reached the addon as `COMMODITY_PURCHASE_FAILED`: of the 10 timed refusals, 10 were `lost` and 0 `error` (`AUCTION_HOUSE_SHOW_ERROR`). `Compra.lua:551-575` records `res = "lost"` and `res = "error"` on the two events. That the text and the event are one refusal is inferred from coincidence (the owner read the text, the addon counted the event). Covers only a purchase that was already pending; an error on the quote was not measured.
- **Record error in the project, not yet corrected:** the first version of the contract (section 13.2, `CONTRATO.md:700-701`) and the diary of `estado.md` (small-hours entry) said the opposite (the text comes through `AUCTION_HOUSE_SHOW_ERROR`). That was a guess written before the measure of 13.4 (`CONTRATO.md:737-738`). The code treats both paths as a lost race, so behaviour did not change. The correction of the project file is proposed to the owner by the main session, not made by this ingest.

### 4. A key bound only at `AUCTION_HOUSE_SHOW` may fail after `/reload` `[product: wow-modder]` (hypothesis, n=1)
- The addon bound the button's key (`SetOverrideBindingClick`) at `AUCTION_HOUSE_SHOW` and tested the auction house by a flag only that event sets. After a `/reload` with the window already open the event does not come, so the key would not bind; the owner saw the space bar "keep jumping". The fix also tests `AuctionHouseFrame:IsShown()` (`Painel.lua:603`, `:609-611`; `Scanner.lua:22` already had the same care).
- **The cause was not isolated:** the first `/hunt` ran on the old version, so a missing `/reload` after the new version is an equally good explanation. The fix covers the second reading. Owner: "usei os dois comandos e minha space bar continua pulando", later "agora funcionou o space".

### 5. One press, one action: no multi-click macro, turbo or held key `[product: wow-modder]` `[owner-preference]` (n=1, forum source)
- The owner asked, in this order, about a Logitech G Hub macro with several left clicks on one button, a held key that clicks by itself, and the mouse software's turbo. All three were declined: the project's fixed limit is no automation, and the reading of the official forums available at the time was "one press, one action". There is no official Blizzard page on one-account macros. The route that stayed: the side mouse button (`BUTTON4`) or a key bound straight to the addon's button, one press per click.
- This is the **project's rule, stricter than the forum reading**, not a Blizzard policy statement. The forum links (`C:\GMS\Claude Code Projects\wow\comum\referencia\forever.md:34`) were not opened by the gate, and detection by Blizzard was not discussed or researched.

### 6. Bait listing and the price floor on Thick Leather (us-111) `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=1 item, realm, night)
- One unit posted below the market was covered within seconds by other sellers, with whole stacks, 1c below and then at the bait's price; many baits were bought at once (the 27 at 4s56c, and by reading most of the 130 at 4s10c). The hourly minimum price stayed **between 5s00c and 8s60c across the 14 snapshots of 25 and 26/11** (7s00c and 8s25c are only the snapshots of 00h26 and 01h26 on 26/11), `node tendencia.mjs --item 4304` in `ferramentas\pregao`.
- "Robots" is the owner's word, not a measure: whether the sellers are bots or people with a posting addon was not measured (the session proposed counting seller names).
- The owner's game rule `[owner-preference]`: up to 3 baits, pause 1 to 2 min if all 3 are bought at once ("uns 40% de sucesso" is his figure). Posting through Blizzard's own screen put 95 units on sale at 5s by accident ("me fudi, sem querer vendi minhas 95 thick leather a 5s cada"); the addon had no sale rule or queue in its SavedVariables, so the addon's bait uses a fixed quantity. Economy is gameplay: the agent did not opine on the strategy.

### 7. Design of the hunt mode `[product: wow-modder]` (n=1 build, contract section 13)
- Each press does one thing: search only the item (one page, the cheap end), quote the quantity from the cheapest lot to the last lot of 3 or more at the target, or confirm. The next search is chained to the press event (purchase done, quote above target, purchase refused), never to a timer. A lot of 1 or 2 alone does not trigger (owner's request) but joins the purchase when it lies below the stack, because a commodity purchase takes from the cheapest upward. Spend cap per hunt (default 5g), a stalled search is redone on the press after 2 s, give up at 3 s (both guesses).
- Bench: two planted mutations (minimum lot 1, quote at double the cost) were caught by the fengari bench; `node ferramentas/guia/pregao/bancada-pregao.mjs --addon addons-proprios/CompraForever --cenarios compra` gave `RESUMO cenarios=23 erros=0` (and `--cenarios rota` 29, 0). The bench was declared by the session; the gate did not reproduce it.
- In the game, the redo of a stalled search at the press (owner, 2026-11-26 about 03h44, 06:44:35Z): "funcionou PERFEITAMENTE, ficou muito mais rápido e responsivo, teve hora que deu internal auction error para 3 ofertas diferentes em 2s quase". No measure of its own. Cycle time of a robot attempt, per the owner, is about 1.5 s of round trip to the server.

### 8. A time-limit test is unstable while the game runs `[product: wow-modder]` (n=1 machine)
- Case P12 of `ferramentas\compra\testar-planejador.mjs` took 1,339, 1,245 and 1,273 ms against a 1,000 ms limit in three runs with WoW open, with no change to `Planejador.lua`. The cause (the game client competing for CPU) is inferred; P12 was not run with the game closed. Same pattern as R19 in [CompraForever Route Modality — Route Computed Outside the Game, Quantities Redone In-Game & a Lua-vs-JS Parity Test](06-compraforever-modalidade-de-rota.md), section 2 (a fixed ms ceiling is fragile on this machine). Evidence: `estado\estado.md`, diary of 26/11 (01h25 to 01h45).
