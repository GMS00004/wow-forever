How the out-of-game tool (`C:\GMS\Claude Code Projects\wow\modder\ferramentas\pregao\`, `legado.mjs` and `estimativas.mjs`) estimates the cost of the Legacy points of the six crafting professions (Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring) up to skill 225, buying everything. ## Key points

### 1. The Naowh Forever recipe table as the source of learn skill and training cost `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=1 build)
- Naowh Forever 1.4.7 has `NaowhForever_Professions\Data\RecipeData.lua`: per recipe, the skill to learn, the origin (trainer, vendor, drop, quest, unknown) and the training cost, "by hand from Wowhead Forever" per the author's README (line 23). In the six professions its colour (yellow, grey) matches the client in 100% of the recipes it covers (98% or more of each); the gate reproduced `divergenciasCor=0`. Training levels: Journeyman 5s, Expert 50s, Artisan 4g 50s in Alchemy and 5g in the others. Its `VendorReagents.lua` lists the 32 reagents a vendor sells without limit; the public vendor list (QuestieDB) includes limited-stock vendors, and the first run bought 79 Peacebloom from one.
- **Scope of the "third-party code is not a reference" rule.** The PregaoForever rule is about code; this is a data file read at runtime from the installed addon, nothing copied into the project. The Naowh addon has no `AGENTS.md` (only Auctionator, Baganator and Syndicator have one; checked on disk by the gate).
- **Owner's ok:** he picked the option "Usar o Naowh (Recomendado)" in a question (2026-11-23 15:36), not a free sentence.
- **Limits.** The data is the addon author's claim, not checked at the trainer. The file changes per Naowh version: the "unknown" origins fell to 347 in 1.4.7 (checked in the file); the 679 in 1.2.18 is only in the transcript (`evidence: perishable`). Evidence: `ferramentas\pregao\naowh.mjs` (`lerRecipeData`, `naowhDaProfissao`, `lerVendorReagents`); `node gerar-receitas.mjs --profissao todas` → `alchemy: ... naowh=176/179 divergenciasCor=0`; `wow\comum\referencia\profissoes-forever.md:162`.

### 2. The greedy route rewarded vendor-profitable recipes and ignored the order book; three corrections, then a fourth `[product: wow-modder]`
- With a point cost of `(cost - vendor sale) x expected crafts`, a recipe that profits at the vendor gets "cheaper" the more crafts it asks, so the 1-to-150 route told the owner to craft 175 Linen Bags (Tailoring) and make 1,626 Light Leather from scraps (Leatherworking). The route also priced by the minimum price while a big purchase climbs the order book.
- Three corrections applied together: the point cost never goes below 0; each craft costs time (10c by default, also for crafts of made reagents); and the calculation is redone with each item at the order-book average price for the quantity the previous route bought, until the route repeats, keeping the best score.
- Result (Leatherworking, `--sem-faixas --tempo-craft 0`, from the transcript, lines 703/806 and 851): -6g 96s 98c with 2,053 crafts before, -2g 51s 82c with 483 crafts after. **The drop from 2,053 to 483 comes from the three corrections together; the effect of each was not isolated.**
- Limits: numbers are from the 23/11 15h25 prices and do not reproduce with another photo; 10c per craft is the agent's choice (about 1g 20s per hour at 20 crafts a minute); the J-window route of Leatherworking still uses the old point cost. Evidence: `CONTRATO.md:706`; `legado.mjs:384` (`custoPontoLegado`), `:443` (`avaliarProfissao`); commit `fdc8d93`. No duplicate in the vault.
- **Fourth correction, 2026-11-23 evening: making an intermediate also raises skill, so the craft count must credit the "package" `[product: WoW Forever]` (n=1 profession checked by hand; source CompraForever 0.3.0 Route Modality, Intermediate Skill Credit & Acceptance Scenarios of Another Author — Session of 2026-11-23 (evening, Avell, wow-modder), item 1 and M1).** The expected-crafts model (`rota.mjs`, linear chance from yellow to grey) counted only the crafts of the step's own recipe; the crafts of a made intermediate (letter F) gave no point. In the Forever client the Heavy Blasting Powder (3945) has yellow 125, grey 145 and 1 point, the same as the Heavy Dynamite (3946), and each Heavy Dynamite takes 2 of it: by the tool's own formula the 144 powders made were almost the whole way from 125 to 145. With the package gain (`chance(main) + sum of k * chance(intermediate)`, only intermediates learnable by the start of the step) the 125-145 band of the Heavy Dynamite, 72 units in report 1801, became 125-140 with 9 units in report 1931 (hand check: 20/3 x (H20 - H5) ~ 8.76), and Engineering 1 to 150 fell from 10g 59s (423 crafts) to 5g 28s (246 crafts). The choice of recipe per band still does not use the credit (only the crafts are redone).
  - **Measurement M1 (same prices, the 18h00 Full):** `legado.mjs --sem-credito` gives the old route (Heavy Dynamite 125-145 x72, Bronze Framework 145-150 x6) and 10g 64s 16c for 1 to 150; with the credit, the new route and 5g 28s 51c. The route change is an effect of the credit (the book repricing redoes the choice with the smaller purchases), so **the whole drop comes from the credit, including the route change it causes**; the earlier "two effects not isolated" doubt is closed.
  - Limits: the chance formula is the Classic one, not checked in Forever; nothing ran in the game; the two M1 reports stayed in the session's temporary folder (`evidence: perishable`), but both commands repeat with the same `precos.json` while it is not rewritten. Evidence: `dados\pregao\receitas-engineering.json` (spells 3945 and 3946); `legado.mjs:453` (`creditarIntermediarios`), `:773` (`--sem-credito`); `dados\pregao\relatorios\legado-225-2026-11-23-1801.md:7`, `:45` and `legado-225-2026-11-23-1931.md:7`, `:44`, `:74`; `ferramentas\pregao\CONTRATO.md:810` (section 10.10, item 4); `node legado.mjs --ate 225 --marcos 150 --profissoes engineering --sem-faixas [--sem-credito]` → `Engineering 1-150 423 10g 64s 16c` and `246 5g 28s 51c`; commit `66d5aef`.

### 4. Redoing the calculation for another house and faction in an isolated copy of the tool `[product: wow-modder]` (new technique, n=1 session, 2026-11-26)
Tied to the lesson of 23/11 that recomputing rewrites the files the addon in use reads (AI-Assisted Work Process, section of 2026-11-23 night; first recorded in CompraForever 0.3.0 Route Modality in the Game — Engineering 1 to 150, a Loose Panel, an Unrequested Recomputation & a Call Interrupted After It Had Written — Session of 2026-11-23 (night, Avell, wow-modder)). This time the owner asked for the new table, so the point is not permission but not touching the real files: `precos.json`, `rotas.json` and the CompraForever `Dados.lua` were not rewritten.
- **The copy.** The whole tool (`ferramentas\pregao\*.mjs`, the `receitas-*.json`, the `cache` and `instalacao.json`) went to the scratchpad in a tree `<root>\modder\ferramentas\pregao` plus `<root>\comum`, because `comum.mjs` finds the data and the installation by the folder of the script itself.
- **Three functional swaps for the Horde.** (1) `CASA_PUBLICA = 'us-113'` in `comum.mjs`; (2) `includes('H')` instead of `includes('A')` at `rota.mjs:232` (a vendor pattern counts only if the faction or a neutral sells it); (3) `auctionator` and `fullCompra` set to null in the copy's `precos.json`, because those scans come from the owner's game and are Alliance.
- **Plus labels (addendum R1, recovered by the gate, checked by the session).** The copy also changed fixed texts in `legado.mjs`: "us-111" became "us-113 (Horda PvP)" and "Alianca" became "Horda" in rule 6 of the report warnings (lines 709, 713, 971, 972). Only labels, but whoever redoes the copy with the three swaps alone gets a report that says "us-111" over another house's prices. In all, 4 files were touched.
- **Faction changed the route.** Pattern: Raptor Hide Harness has `lados: ["H"]` in Naowh and entered the Horde Leatherworking route; the Alliance route uses Pattern: Barbaric Leggings.
- **The Alliance Normal copy serves only as a check:** it came out identical to the original (empty diff), same photo of 15h26. The real files did not change (`precos.json` 12:39, `rotas.json` and `Dados.lua` 12:44, unchanged after both runs).
- **Limits.** n=1 session. The copy has no test of its own (`legado.mjs` ran with exit 0 in both). The tool gained no house or faction option: redoing needs the copy assembled again. The scratchpad can be erased, so the label diff reproduces only while the copy exists. Evidence: `C:\GMS\Claude Code Projects\wow\modder\ferramentas\pregao\rota.mjs:232`; `comum.mjs:16` (`CASA_PUBLICA`), `:10`-`:11` (`RAIZ_MODDER`, `DADOS`), `:43` (`instalacao.json` by `RAIZ_MODDER`); `estado\estado.md:264`; reports `dados\pregao\relatorios\legado-225-horda-pvp-2026-11-26-1556.md` ("Padroes a comprar: Pattern: Raptor Hide Harness (vendedor, 25s 00c)"; header "foto publica do leilao us-113 (Horda PvP)") and `legado-225-alianca-normal-2026-11-26-1608.md` ("Pattern: Barbaric Leggings"); `HANDOFF-custo-legacy-profissoes.md:117-118` ("Recalcular regrava os arquivos que o addon em uso lê: só com o ok dele"); `ls -la` of the three real files.
